How to play:

Players take turns saying words beginning with the last letter of the previous word (in last word rules), or the next letter alphabetically (in alphabetical). The word must be somehow related to the previous word, though the extent to which it can be related is up to the players (e.g. "apple" and "banana" are both fruits, so they are related).
